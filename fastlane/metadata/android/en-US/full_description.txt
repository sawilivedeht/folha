Folha is an e-book reader for people who just want to read: no account, no ads and no tracking. Your books stay on your device.

The interface is in Brazilian Portuguese.

Formats
• EPUB and TXT/Markdown.
• PDF, converted into reflowable text that fits the screen. Scanned PDFs become one image per page, as there is no OCR.

Reading
• Turn pages by tapping, swiping or with the keyboard, with a progress bar and the time left in the chapter.
• Aa panel: font size, line spacing, margins, font family, justification and hyphenation.
• Light, sepia, dark and black themes.
• Table of contents, bookmarks and automatically saved progress.

Privacy
• Works fully offline: the app requests no permissions, not even internet access.
• Android cloud backup is disabled, so your library never leaves the device.
